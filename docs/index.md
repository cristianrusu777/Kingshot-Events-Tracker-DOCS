## What you can learn here

Ralyvora Docs explains how people use Ralyvora platforms, what information each workflow needs, what the platform produces, and what to try when something does not work. Kingshot Events is the only platform currently documented.

## Product areas

Kingshot Events covers scoped player records, event participation and scores, imports, analytics and rewards, Castle Position workflows, Knowledge Hub content, subscriptions, and planning tools in the Lab.

## Independent community platform

Ralyvora is an independent community platform. It is not officially affiliated with or endorsed by Kingshot or its publisher.
