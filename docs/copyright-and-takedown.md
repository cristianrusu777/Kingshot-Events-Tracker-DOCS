# Copyright and Takedown
Use the current support or contact route in Kingshot Events to report content that may infringe rights or expose information that should not be public. Identify the exact article or documentation URL, the material at issue, and a safe way to follow up.

Do not include unrelated personal data or credentials. Reports are reviewed; submission does not by itself determine the outcome.
