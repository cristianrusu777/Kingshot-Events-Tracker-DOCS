# Reading and Finding Knowledge

**Knowledge Hub** contains game guides, heroes, events, mechanics, databases, and community articles. It is the product's reading library; this documentation explains how to use it.

## Find an article

Open Knowledge Hub from public or signed-in navigation. Browse the homepage, category sections, entity pages, or a community space. Use search for a title, phrase, hero, event, or mechanic. Search results respect current visibility, so signing in or changing community context can change what is returned.

Article cards can expose a title, summary, category, space, publication or access badge, and updated information. Open the result to read the current published revision. Draft and archived revisions do not replace the public article until the editorial workflow publishes them.

## Spaces and visibility

Public spaces are available without community membership. Scoped spaces can belong to a kingdom or alliance and require the corresponding account context. Premium content can show a teaser or locked state when the effective plan lacks access. Knowing a direct URL does not bypass any of these checks.

## Reading experience

Articles are built from structured content blocks such as headings, text, lists, callouts, tables, media, references, or other supported elements. Use the page outline and related links to move through long articles. Media captions, alt text, and source context help explain what an image represents.

When Reading Verification is assigned, a small reader-specific marker appears inside the ordinary article. Continue reading normally and use the session form at the end. See [Reading Verification](/kingshot-events/knowledge-hub/reading-verification).

## Purpose, controls, and worked result

Knowledge search helps readers find the canonical published article they are permitted to know exists. Controls include search terms, category or space, source language, and visible article metadata. The decision order keeps unpublished material out of ordinary results, then applies identity, scope, and premium access. **Example:** A signed-out reader searches a premium title and receives the permitted teaser. After sign-in with effective access, the same slug opens full blocks. A members-only alliance article outside the reader's space remains unavailable rather than advertised. Search cannot bypass state or access. Troubleshoot with title, slug, search terms, identity state, active scope, access label, and exact message.

## More comfortable reading and discovery

Long guides have a collapsible table of contents, and **Print or save as PDF** opens the browser's print workflow for the article you can currently read. Preview the output before saving or sharing; a printable copy is not permission to redistribute members-only content.

Articles can appear in more than one relevant category. Hero and Master database entries provide structured game information alongside ordinary guides. Supported tool or simulator widgets can appear inside an article, bringing a relevant calculation closer to its explanation.

Reader navigation, menus, and overlays have been refined for smaller screens and both colour themes. Author images use the account avatar where available. Publication and access rules still apply when following a category, printing, or opening a direct link.

Eligible premium reading can come from a [personal plan](/kingshot-events/subscriptions/personal-plans), but private community material still has its own access requirements. An account's name styling is presentation, not an access badge.

## If an older layout looks different

Mobile menus, dropdown overlays, loading presentation, the article outline, statistics presentation, and space labels have been refined. Use the current category, space label, and table of contents to orient yourself. These changes do not publish a draft or make a protected article public. See the [fixed and replaced items](/kingshot-events/updates/release-notes#fixed) for the wider update.
