# Problems Kingshot Events Solves
Kingshot communities often receive information in different formats and at different times. Kingshot Events gives authorized people a shared place to organize that information while keeping it connected to its kingdom and alliance.

## From scattered records to reviewed information

The platform can turn supported screenshots into proposed structured rows, accept manual entries, and collect records in reviewable workflows. Extracted information is a starting point: people must verify names, dates, scores, participation, and scope before saving.

## From applications to a published schedule

Castle Position tools connect player choices with manager review and schedule preparation. A submitted application is not an appointment. Users follow its status until an authorized manager publishes a schedule.

## From raw records to useful views

Analytics and reward views summarize saved information. Their usefulness depends on the selected scope, available event records, configured rules, and the viewer's access.

## From private know-how to shared guides

Knowledge Hub supports public, community-scoped, and access-limited articles. Authors draft content and authorized reviewers decide what is published.

## Planning, not promises

Lab tools model supported inputs for gear, charms, rallies, battles, and Bear Trap scenarios. Results are estimates based on the selected data and assumptions; they do not guarantee in-game outcomes.
