# Product Terminology
## Core records

**Scope** is the kingdom or alliance context currently selected. **Player** is a governor record managed in that context. **Event template** is a reusable event definition; **event instance** is one occurrence. **Result** is a saved participation or score outcome.

## Workflow states

**Draft** is still being prepared. **Saved** means information was stored but may not be submitted or published. **Submitted** means it entered a review workflow. **Reviewed** means an authorized person assessed it. **Published** means the approved result is visible to its intended audience. **Archived** keeps historical content outside normal active views. **Soft-deleted** means removed from ordinary lists but potentially restorable by an authorized workflow.

## Community roles

**Alliance leader** and **co-leader** manage an alliance scope when assigned. **King** or **kingdom manager** works across the assigned kingdom. **Minister of Justice** is a scoped community role used for relevant kingdom workflows. **Knowledge author** prepares content but does not necessarily have publication authority.

## Product areas

**Knowledge Hub** contains guides and structured community content. **Reading Verification** records whether a reader submits the small fragment assigned within an article session. **Lab** is the group of simulators, optimizers, profiles, and reference data.
