# Your First Visit
Start on the public home, sign in if your work requires an account, and read any availability banner. Public Knowledge Hub pages, Castle Position application entry points, and enabled Lab tools may be available without a session.

## First steps

1. Sign in or register when the task needs saved, scoped information.
2. Open **Profile** and confirm your account details and linked player information.
3. Check which navigation groups are visible. Their contents reflect your role and enabled modules.
4. Confirm the active kingdom or alliance before changing shared records.
5. Begin with a personal page such as **My Analytics**, **My Rewards**, or **My Appointments**, or with a manager workspace appropriate to your assignment.

If a page is absent, do not assume data was deleted. Check your account, scope, access assignment, subscription, and feature availability.
