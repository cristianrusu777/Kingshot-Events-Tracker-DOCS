# Subscription Access, Scope, and Usage
Subscription access belongs to the scope shown in the plan or grant. A kingdom entitlement can allocate access to eligible alliances; a direct alliance subscription applies to that alliance. Do not assume one alliance's access transfers to another.

Usage and quota warnings indicate the current plan state. A warning does not necessarily mean saved data is lost. Suspension or expiry can make premium actions unavailable while records remain subject to normal retention and access behavior.

Check the effective plan, scope, and visible usage before opening a support request.
