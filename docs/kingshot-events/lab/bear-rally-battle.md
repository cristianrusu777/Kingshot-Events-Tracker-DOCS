# Bear Trap, Rally, and Battle Simulators
**Bear Trap Simulator** models captains, joiners, widgets, Valora, formation, and damage uncertainty. **Rally Simulator** explains captain, joiner, skill, widget, formation, and stacking effects for offense or defense. **Battle Simulator** compares two configured sides across repeatable modeled runs.

Enter both the scenario and the environment carefully. For Bear Trap, check formation and participant assumptions. For rallies, check captain and joiner roles. For battles, configure both sides and use the run controls provided.

Beta outputs can change as supported data and models are refined. They are estimates, not official formulas or guaranteed outcomes.
